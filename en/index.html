Maison Carla · Rue Masséna

Yesterday’s character, today’s comfort

Maison Carla grew out of Carlà’s love for this very special city, for “Nissa la bella”, whose unique blend of sea, light, cosmopolitan history and cultural and architectural heritage creates an elegant, lively atmosphere found nowhere else.

Fully renovated with attention to detail, the home reflects a desire to feel in tune with the city. Olive green, ecru and mustard, wood and brass accents create a welcoming interior that preserves the original spirit of the building’s maison bourgeoise, with a nod to mid-century furnishings and the comforts of today.

On the third floor, the apartment enjoys beautiful light through its large windows and overlooks rue Masséna, in one of Nice’s most elegant and central neighbourhoods. The preserved original wrought-iron railing frames the life of the street, while open windows welcome the sea breeze.

Step outside and you are already in the heart of the city: Place Masséna, the green Promenade du Paillon, the shops of Avenue Jean Médecin, boutiques, central streets and the waterfront become part of your stay.

Living room and kitchen

A space to enjoy together

The sofa, the 1950s-style armchair and the dining area offer a place to come together after a day in the city, perhaps over an aperitif before choosing one of the restaurants on rue Masséna or along the waterfront, just a short walk away. Large windows and carefully chosen furnishings give the living room its character, with a Smart TV for your enjoyment.

The new, modern kitchen has an induction hob, microwave, fridge with freezer compartment and dishwasher. A dedicated retro corner brings together a Nespresso machine, kettle and toaster, so breakfast feels just like home. If you would prefer a French-style breakfast, you can visit one of the neighbourhood’s pastry shops.

Main bedroom

The pleasure of a good night’s sleep

A bright bedroom with soft colours, warm lighting and a quality 160 × 200 cm double bed. Blackout curtains let you control the light, while a desk is always available for a little personal space during your travels.

Second bedroom

One room, different possibilities

The second bedroom can be prepared with two 80 × 200 cm single beds or with the beds joined in a 160 × 200 cm double arrangement.

Matisse-inspired prints, green textiles and a large window carry the style of the home into this room. Please specify your preferred arrangement when confirming your booking.

The apartment accommodates up to four guests, with sleeping places in the two bedrooms.

Workspaces

Room for your projects, too

For writing, reading or a few hours of work, Maison Carla has a desk in the main bedroom that is always available.

On request, a second pull-out desk can be set up in the second bedroom. In this arrangement, one sleeping place remains in that room and the apartment accommodates a total of three guests.

Both workspaces offer natural light, power sockets within reach and Wi-Fi.

Bathroom

Thoughtful materials and everyday comfort

A spacious shower with a rainfall showerhead, elegant zellige tiles and warm finishes give the bathroom its character, with the same attention to detail as the rest of the home. Toiletries are provided for guests.

A washing machine, iron and ironing board are also available: useful comforts for longer stays.

Nice.

Carrè d’Or

Nice, right outside your door

Maison Carla is on rue Masséna in the lively Carrè d’Or. Fashion boutiques, food shops, restaurants and small stores line your walks through the centre, with Place Masséna just a few steps away.

From here, exploring Nice on foot feels natural: walk to the sea, wander the Old Town’s lanes, browse the markets and return home easily between visits. Nearby shops have everything for a delicious aperitif at home, or you can choose from the many quality restaurants just a short stroll from your door.

The beach and the Promenade des Anglais retain their charm in autumn and winter, when the light over the Baie des Anges reveals another side of the Riviera.

Private garage included

The convenience of leaving the car

A private enclosed garage is included in your stay at no additional cost.

It is at Résidence Donatello, with access from rue Blacas, approximately 550 metres from the apartment in a separate building. A practical way to leave the car and explore central Nice on foot.

A personal welcome

A private concierge service welcomes you on arrival, hands over the keys and introduces you to the home and its facilities. If you use the garage, they accompany you to show you the entrance.

Good to know

Maison Carla accommodates up to four guests, or three when the second desk is set up.

The apartment has air conditioning in every room and automated infrared panel heating.

There is a lift, but it does not stop at the apartment’s floor: access requires going up and down a few steps.

The apartment is not suitable for children under six or guests with reduced mobility. Pets are not allowed. Smoking is not permitted.
